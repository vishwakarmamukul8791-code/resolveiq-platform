Incident ID: INC-1001
Category: Email Service
Priority: High
Status: Resolved

Issue:
Users were unable to send or receive emails through Microsoft Outlook.

Root Cause:
The Exchange Transport Service stopped unexpectedly after a recent Windows security update.

Resolution:
1. Restarted the Microsoft Exchange Transport Service.
2. Verified service dependencies.
3. Checked mail queue and confirmed message delivery.
4. Monitored service for 30 minutes.

Keywords:
Outlook, Exchange Server, Email, Transport Service
